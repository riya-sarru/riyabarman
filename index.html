Case Studies ✦

Real Projects. Real Impact.

A closer look at how I think, create and solve content challenges.

01
Healthy Jeena Sikho

Making Healthcare Content More Relatable

Objective

Improve low social media views and reach.

Research & Observation

Healthcare content was often educational, heavy and overly serious. I analyzed other companies, the audience and how content was being received.

Gap Identified

The educational value was useful, but the presentation needed a more relatable and engaging element.

Strategy

Keep the useful information while introducing lighter, more engaging ways to communicate around products such as wheelchairs, hospital beds and oxygen cylinders.

Execution

Planned content around audience needs, hooks, captions, descriptions and more engaging video messaging.

Performance

People started watching more, and reach, views, likes and followers began improving.

Learning

Healthcare content doesn't always have to feel heavy. Useful information can be presented in a relatable and engaging way.

02
Employee Engagement

Turning Employee Engagement into Social Media Content

Objective

Increase employee engagement while creating relatable social media content.

Challenge

Long working hours and work pressure meant employees had limited interaction and there was little employee-led fun content.

Strategy

Use content creation itself as a way to give employees a break, help them interact and involve them in the creative process.

Execution

Planned small games, shoots and fun content. Employees who were initially uncomfortable on camera were gradually encouraged to participate.

Performance

One employee-led reel achieved 20,000+ reach, and overall content reach started coming in above 20,000.

Impact

Employees enjoyed participating, while communication, acting and content-making skills also improved.

Learning

Good content doesn't always start with a trend. Sometimes it starts with people.

03
Exclusive Gifts × MV Cosmetics

Turning Two Products into One Giftable Concept

Objective

Combine gifting and makeup into a creative concept that positioned makeup as a gift.

Strategy

Instead of simply promoting a product, build a story around the idea: if makeup is to be gifted, how can it be presented as a thoughtful gift?

Execution

Developed the collaboration concept “MV Gifts”, created the story and script, planned product presentation and acted in the shoot.

Result

The concept helped audiences see makeup as a gifting option through Exclusive Gifts, and affiliate-based sales followed.

Contribution

Creative ideation, storytelling, script development, collaboration concept, content planning and shoot execution.

Learning

Sometimes creativity is not about creating a completely new product — it's about presenting an existing product in a completely new way.

✦ How I Work

How I Work ♡

Behind every piece of content, there’s a thought. ♡
I don’t start with “What should we post today?”
I start with “Why would someone want to stop and watch this?” ♥
Before creating anything, I try to understand the brand, the audience, the purpose and the problem we are trying to solve.

Sometimes that means noticing that healthcare content has become too serious. Sometimes it means turning employee interaction into content people actually enjoy. And sometimes, it means looking at two completely different products and thinking, “What if we tell one story with both?” ♡
That’s how I approach content.→ Start with WHY.
→ Find the story.
→ Make people care.
→ See what happens.
→ Do it better next time. ☺
